**HTTPAPI Internal Server Disclosure**

**CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:N/A:N = 5.3 (Medium)**

### Why this score

* **AV:N** – the information is exposed over the network
* **AC:L** – no special conditions are needed beyond reaching the service
* **PR:N** – no authentication was required
* **UI:N** – no user interaction is needed
* **S:U** – impact stays within the same scope
* **C:L** – the service reveals internal server fingerprints, service headers, hostnames, and that an internal HTTPAPI service is present
* **I:N / A:N** – no integrity or availability impact was demonstrated

### Description

Multiple Windows servers exposed **Microsoft HTTPAPI/2.0** services on management-related ports such as **47001** and **5985**. The service responses returned **404 Not Found** pages with the `Microsoft-HTTPAPI/2.0` server header, confirming that internal HTTP services are reachable and fingerprintable from the testing network. In several cases, these HTTPAPI services were part of broader internal management or relay surfaces, increasing reconnaissance value for an attacker.
An attacker can use the disclosed HTTPAPI service details to perform targeted reconnaissance against the environment. Exposure of internal service information such as Microsoft HTTPAPI usage, management ports, and backend infrastructure fingerprints assists attackers in identifying underlying technologies, management interfaces, and potential attack paths for further exploitation. Although no direct compromise was identified during testing, such information disclosure reduces the attack complexity for subsequent attacks including service enumeration, vulnerability mapping, and targeted exploitation attempts against exposed management services.


### Steps to Reproduce

1. From Kali, run Nmap or curl against the exposed HTTPAPI ports on any affected server.
2. Example:

   ```bash
   nmap -Pn -sC -sV -p47001,5985 <ip>
   ```

   or

   ```bash
   curl -v http://<ip>:47001/
   curl -v http://<ip>:5985/
   ```
3. Observe that the service responds with:

   * `Microsoft-HTTPAPI/2.0`
   * `HTTP/1.1 404 Not Found` or similar response
4. Confirm that the service is exposed without requiring prior authentication.

### Resolution

* Restrict access to internal HTTPAPI/management services using network segmentation and firewall rules.
* Expose WinRM and internal HTTPAPI endpoints only to authorized administrative hosts.
* Remove or reduce unnecessary service banner disclosure where possible.
* Ensure management interfaces are not reachable from untrusted network segments.
