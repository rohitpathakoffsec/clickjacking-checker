Song Prompt

Style: Acoustic Pop / Indie Pop

Tempo: 110–120 BPM

Vocals: Warm male or female lead with bright, friendly delivery

Instruments: Acoustic guitar, light piano, soft percussion, subtle bass, cheerful claps, and light background harmonies

Vibe: Optimistic, refreshing, cozy, morning motivation, coffee-shop atmosphere

Chorus should be catchy and memorable, with a sing-along feel. The song should make listeners smile and feel energized, like the first sip of coffee on a beautiful morning.
