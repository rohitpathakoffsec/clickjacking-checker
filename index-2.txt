## 6) Expired SSL/TLS Certificate

### Proper Title

**Expired SSL/TLS Certificate Identified on HTTPS Service**

### Active CWE

**CWE-295: Improper Certificate Validation**

### STRIDE Mapping

**4 - Information Disclosure**
**1 - Spoofing Identity**

### CVSS v3.1

**CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:L/A:N = 6.5 (Medium)**

### Why this score

* **AV:N** – remotely exploitable over the network
* **AC:L** – no special attack complexity required
* **PR:N** – no authentication required
* **UI:N** – users may unknowingly trust invalid certificates
* **S:U** – impact remains within same scope
* **C:L** – expired certificates weaken trust validation and confidentiality assurance
* **I:L** – increases risk of spoofing or interception attacks
* **A:N** – no direct availability impact observed

### Affected Asset

* **nstm95035nap.eng.wintel.ubseng.net**

  * Port: `443/tcp`

### Description

The HTTPS service exposed an expired SSL/TLS certificate during service enumeration. The certificate validity period observed during testing was:

```text id="8byj8n"
Not valid before: 2019-03-23T19:07:29
Not valid after: 2021-03-22T19:07:29
```

The service continued to present the expired certificate to connecting clients.

### Impact

Expired SSL/TLS certificates weaken the trust relationship between clients and the affected service. Attackers may exploit expired or improperly maintained certificates to facilitate spoofing, interception, or man-in-the-middle style attacks, particularly where users ignore browser or client certificate warnings. Additionally, expired certificates indicate poor certificate lifecycle management and may result in reduced trustworthiness of administrative or application services exposed by the environment.

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Run SSL enumeration against the target host:

   ```bash
   nmap -Pn -sV --script ssl-cert -p443 <ip>
   ```

2. Observe the certificate validity dates returned by the service.

3. Verify that the certificate expiration date has passed.

4. Alternatively:

   ```bash
   openssl s_client -connect <ip>:443
   ```

5. Review the certificate validity period.

### Resolution

* Replace the expired SSL/TLS certificate with a valid trusted certificate.
* Implement certificate lifecycle monitoring and automated renewal processes.
* Periodically review externally exposed certificates for expiration and trust status.
* Remove deprecated or unused HTTPS services where operationally unnecessary.
